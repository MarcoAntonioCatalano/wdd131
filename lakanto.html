Receiving

Regular Orders

  • Step 1

    Pull INR xxxxxx in ACU. Make sure that it says pick complete. If not shipped message through “Internal Transfer” chat asking to ship the INR #.

  • Step 2

    Add INR to KORBER (Receiving Icon) adding “TO” in front of the INR. Go to pack slip and filter (contains), Add the INR with TO in front. The INR should show if it was shipped. If the INR says suspended it means it has a hold. Then go to the “I” icon, because it needs to be inspected. Once the inspection is done, the suspended hold should be removed.

  • Step 3

    Go to Korber Edge to receive the INR.

  • * Click on Receiving
  • * Regular
  • * Enter INR adding TO in front
  • * Enter product SKU
    • It might ask for LOT #, Expiration date and location (BIN) . When it asks for cases, hit skip.

  • Step 4

    Once received check Korber Inventory

  • * Filter BIN # destination

  • Step 5

    Go back to receiving, hit refresh and make sure it uploaded. It should dissapear once it uploads. If it doesn't upload, click on jobs, complete PO, finish. This should force the upload, thus giving you a receipt # on ACU.

  • Step 6

    Fill out “Incoming Material Inspection Form” 2nd sheet. Make sure a supervision signs it. This sheet should also have a time stamp when it was received.

  • Step 7

    Fill out “Receiving KPI Tracker” form.

  • Step 8

    Scan forms and send to your email, then save under files with its corresponding INR. Then drag from files to ACU under the respective INR that was received.

  • Step 9

    Lastly, check under ACU of the processed INR if the receipt was created. If it was, that should conclude the process, if not, create a new receipt on ACU under tab RECEIPT/ NEW adding the INR. Make sure location says “SUS1.” then click save.
